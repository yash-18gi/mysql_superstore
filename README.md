# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore sales dataset using **MySQL** to understand business performance and identify factors affecting sales and profitability.

The analysis focuses on **sales, profit, customers, products, categories, regions, discounts, and shipping performance**.

The main objective is to identify profitable and loss-making areas of the business and generate **data-driven insights and recommendations** that can help improve profitability and operational efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Superstore generates significant sales, but sales alone do not indicate whether the business is performing well.

Some products, customers, categories, regions, and highly discounted orders may contribute less profit or even generate losses.

Therefore, the business needs to understand:

- Which categories and sub-categories are most profitable?
- Which products and orders are generating losses?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which customers generate the most sales?
- Which regions perform better?
- How are sales changing over time?
- How efficient is the shipping process?
- Which products are the top performers within each category?

This project uses SQL to answer these questions and provide meaningful business insights.
